When should caregivers be alerted about blood pressure?
TIHM dataset · 56 patients living with dementia · 1 Apr – 30 Jun 2019. Rules are applied to each day's average reading.
Missed = a recorded BP event-day the rule did not flag. Alert, no recorded event is not necessarily a false alarm: TIHM events were recorded by a monitoring team and are not a complete record. Retrospective exploration only; matching past events does not show that a rule is clinically safe.
